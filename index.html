National Best-Practice Award

Digital Transformation of the Retire and Return Process

Directed the complete digital overhaul of a legacy, paper-based "retire-and-return" pathway that previously suffered from misplaced documentation, poor cross-departmental communication, and low user satisfaction (71% user difficulty; 32% manager satisfaction). Transformed the manual workflow into a streamlined, transparent, and user-friendly automated system.

The Challenge

Misplaced paper forms, 71% of staff finding the process complicated, 32% manager satisfaction, and lack of cross-departmental visibility between HR, Payroll, and Clinical teams.

Technical Actions

Mapped end-to-end workflows via stakeholder sessions; engineered digital intake with MS Forms; configured SharePoint status worklists; implemented multi-stage automated routing and email triggers in Power Automate; authored staff FAQs and guidance flowcharts.

Measurable Outcomes & Impact
  • 100% Trackable Workflow: Eliminated lost paperwork entirely and accelerated approval cycle times.
  • National Recognition: Published by NHS Employers as a national best-practice case study.
  • Internal Excellence Award: Received Trust organizational awards for digital innovation and staff satisfaction.

TempRE Vacancy Approvals System (Bank Staff)

Developed and implemented an end-to-end multi-stage digital system to automate the TempRE vacancy approval process using Agile methodology. Replaced an inefficient paper process to create a robust, auditable trail and resolve persistent issues with missing data, lost forms, and unapproved shifts.

The Challenge

Disjointed hand-offs between Requesting Departments, TSS, GDO, and DCMO; frequent shift data discrepancies; missing audit logs for medical vacancies.

Technical Actions

Mapped end-to-end handoffs; digitized vacancy submissions with MS Forms; programmed conditional multi-tier approval logic in Power Automate (standard, urgent, amended); established centralized SharePoint list logs; created automated status notifications to requesters and TSS.

Measurable Outcomes & Impact
  • 2,500+ Requests Audited: Centralized real-time logging across all clinical departments with complete decision audit trail.
  • Zero Unapproved Shifts Published: Automatic confirmation triggers to TSS ensure only fully approved vacancies enter TempRE.
  • Iterative Agile Improvements: Conducted iterative user testing to optimize usability and quality of life for clinical managers.
